Last updated: May 9, 2026

LottoIQ operates the website located at lottoiq.ca. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal information when you visit our website, create an account, use our tools, contact us, or purchase a subscription.

LottoIQ is a Canadian lottery analytics platform. We provide data-driven tools such as number generation, historical frequency analysis, hot and cold number tracking, and pattern statistics. We do not claim to predict winning lottery numbers. By using LottoIQ, you acknowledge that we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal information as described in this Privacy Policy.

  • Name

  • Billing information
  • Customer support messages
  • Feedback, reviews, or survey responses
  • Subscription and account preferences

We may also collect technical and usage information automatically when you use our website, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type

  • Pages visited

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.
